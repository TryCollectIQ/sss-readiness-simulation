Discrete-time queueing model, 25 Monte Carlo replications, 365-day horizon. Backlogs are reported at peak with the day of occurrence, and each stage reports the number of days it ran at capacity, which is the unambiguous signal of a throughput constraint. A queue that forms and drains, or a stage that runs at capacity and then idles once the target is met, is invisible in a terminal-day or full-horizon reading. Every rate and capacity shown is a placeholder for Government-furnished planning data; the model structure, not the numbers, is what this prototype demonstrates. Production implementation targets Python/SimPy, containerized for the SSS-designated cloud environment, delivered with full source, IaC, and a documented V&V plan under Government ownership.
